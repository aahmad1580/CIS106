M8P1 - Five Years of Compound Interest
Input: Principal amount and annual interest rate as a decimal
Processing: Start with the principal as the balance. Repeat for years 1
            through 5. Calculate interest = beginning balance * rate.
            Add the interest to the balance for the next year. After the
            loop, subtract the original principal from the final balance.
Output: Year, beginning balance, ending balance, and total interest earned

M8P2 - Fibonacci Sequence
Input: None
Processing: Start with two values of 1. Repeat 20 times. Display the first
            value, add the two values, and shift to the second value and sum.
Output: The first 20 Fibonacci numbers, starting with 1 and 1

M8P3 - Employee Bonuses
Input: Employee last names and salaries from M8P3_Employees.txt
Processing: Read each name and its salary. Use 20% for salaries of at
            least $100,000, 15% for exactly $50,000, and 10% otherwise.
            Multiply salary by the bonus rate and add the bonus to the total.
Output: Each employee's last name, salary, and bonus, then total bonuses paid

M8P4 - Order Totals
Input: Item names, quantities, and unit prices from M8P4_Orders.txt
Processing: Read each item, quantity, and price. Multiply quantity by price
            for the extended price. Add it to the running total and add one
            to the order count. Divide the total by the count if there are
            any orders. If the file is empty, show an average of $0.00.
Output: Each item, quantity, price, and extended price, then the total of
        extended prices, number of orders, and average order

M8P5 - Student Tuition
Input: Student last names, district codes, and credits from M8P5_Students.txt
Processing: Read each student's information. Use $250 per credit for I
            (in district) and $500 for O (out of district). Multiply credits
            by the rate, add the tuition to the running total, and count the
            student.
Output: Each student's last name, credits, and tuition owed, then total
        tuition owed and number of students
