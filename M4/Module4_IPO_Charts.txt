M4P1 - Weighted Exam Score
Input: First exam score, second exam score
Processing: total_score = exam1 * 0.60 + exam2 * 0.40
Output: Total score

M4P2 - Stock Value Change
Input: Purchase price per share, current price, quantity
Processing: value_change = (current_price - purchase_price) * quantity
Output: Increase or decrease in stock value

M4P3 - Meal Tips
Input: Meal total
Processing: Multiply meal total by 0.15, 0.18, and 0.20; add each tip to meal total
Output: Meal total, tip, total with tip for each rate

M4P4 - Calories Burned
Input: First name, steps walked
Processing: calories_burned = steps * 0.25
Output: First name, calories burned

M4P5 - Break-Even Point
Input: Fixed costs, price per unit, cost per unit
Processing: break_even = fixed_costs / (price_per_unit - cost_per_unit)
Output: Break-even point in units
