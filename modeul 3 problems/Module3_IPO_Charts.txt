Input | Processing | Output
Make, model, MSRP, discount as a decimal | amount_off = msrp * discount_percent; discounted_price = msrp - amount_off | Make, model, MSRP, discount percent, amount off, discounted price

Radius | area = 3.14 * radius * radius; perimeter = 2 * 3.14 * radius | Area, perimeter
